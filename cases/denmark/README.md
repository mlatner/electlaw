# Denmark — local elections

Municipal and regional elections under the *Lov om kommunale og
regionale valg*. Flexible list with strong list-cartel
(*listeforbund* / *valgforbund*) tradition: lists can declare joint
allocation pools at three levels (within a list, between lists in a
cartel, between cartels in a super-cartel).

## Open questions to resolve

- Formation of a *kandidatliste* outside a registered party: signature
  / supporter requirements at municipal level.
- *Sideordnet opstilling* vs. *partiliste* — the choice of intra-list
  allocation rule (parallel vs. ordered) and who makes it.
- Statutory text on *listeforbund* and *valgforbund* — exact mechanics,
  who can join with whom, public disclosure.
- D'Hondt application across the cartel structure.
- Practical incidence of pure local lists vs. national-party branches.
