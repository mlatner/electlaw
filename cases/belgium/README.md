# Belgium — local elections

Municipal elections governed by regional law since the regionalization
of local government — *Vlaams Gemeentedecreet* / Flemish Local and
Provincial Elections Decree, Walloon *Code de la démocratie locale et
de la décentralisation*, Brussels-Capital separately. Flexible-list PR.

## Open questions to resolve

- Differences between the three regional regimes on list submission,
  signature counts, and deposit.
- Local-only lists (*lokale lijsten* / *listes locales*) — how
  recognized, and how common.
- Compulsory voting interaction with list dynamics.
- The Flemish abolition of the "list vote" (*lijststem*) for municipal
  elections (effective 2024) — what did it change in practice, and what
  is the new intra-list seat assignment rule?
- Threshold and allocation method (Imperiali at municipal level — note
  the divergence from D'Hondt).

## Primary sources to acquire

- Flanders: *Lokaal en Provinciaal Kiesdecreet*.
- Wallonia: *Code de la démocratie locale*, Livre I, Titre IV.
- Brussels: *Code électoral communal bruxellois*.
