# Netherlands — local elections

Municipal council (*gemeenteraad*) elections under the *Kieswet*.
Open-list PR with preference votes. **Local-only parties (*lokale
partijen*)** are extremely common and frequently win pluralities; this
is one of the strongest working examples of a non-national-party list
ecosystem.

## Open questions to resolve

- Registration of a list (*lijst*) for municipal elections: signature
  requirements (*ondersteuningsverklaringen*), deposit, deadlines.
- The *blanco lijst* mechanism — listing without an official party name.
- Preference-vote threshold (currently 25% of the *kiesdeler*) — how it
  reorders the list, exact statutory language.
- Treatment of pure-local lists vs. national-party local branches in
  funding and broadcast access.
- Apparentement (*lijstverbinding*) — was it abolished for municipal
  elections, when, and what replaced it?

## Primary sources to acquire

- *Kieswet*, especially chapters on candidacy (Hoofdstuk H) and
  allocation (Hoofdstuk P).
- *Kiesraad* guidance for municipal elections.
