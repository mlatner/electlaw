# United Kingdom — local elections

**Caveat**: the UK is largely a *negative* case for this project. English
local elections are FPTP. The PR experience is concentrated in venues we
have excluded (Scottish local STV since 2007, Northern Ireland local STV)
or in regimes that have been abolished (Supplementary Vote for elected
mayors, repealed for English mayors in 2022).

## Why include it anyway

- **London Assembly (Additional Member System / MMP)** is the closest
  thing in the UK to our system of interest, and is governed by the
  *Greater London Authority Act 1999*. Worth a careful read on list
  submission, ballot access, and how independents/non-party lists fare.
- The UK also illustrates the **administrative consequences of not
  having a non-partisan list infrastructure**: independents stand as
  individuals, never on lists, even where lists exist.
