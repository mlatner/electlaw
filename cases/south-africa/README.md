# South Africa — local elections

Municipal council elections under the *Local Government: Municipal
Electoral Act, 2000* and the *Local Government: Municipal Structures
Act, 1998*. **Mixed system at local level**: roughly half ward seats
(FPTP) plus half PR list seats, designed so total council composition
is proportional. This is a working example of MMP-style design at the
local tier and the most direct US analog among our cases.

Independents may now contest **both ward and PR list seats**
following the *Electoral Amendment Act, 2023* (in response to the
*New Nation Movement* Constitutional Court judgment) — making South
Africa unusually relevant to the non-partisan-list question.

## Open questions to resolve

- Statutory mechanics for an independent candidate to access the PR
  list ballot at municipal level: signatures, deposits, geographic
  spread.
- How a single-name "list" of one independent is treated for allocation
  purposes (formula, surplus, threshold).
- Compensatory mechanics: precise statutory formula for translating
  ward results + list votes into final council composition.
- Treatment of vacancies on an independent's seat (no list to draw
  from).
- Lessons from the 2024 national / provincial implementation that bear
  on local administration.
