# Finland — local elections

Municipal elections under the *Vaalilaki* (Election Act, 714/1998).
**Pure open list**: voters cast a single vote for an individual
candidate, and that vote also counts for the candidate's list. Intra-
list seat assignment is determined entirely by personal vote totals —
the list submitter does not set a binding order.

This is the strongest "candidate-centric list" model and is often
overlooked in US comparative discussions.

## Open questions to resolve

- *Valitsijayhdistys* — voter associations as the legal vehicle for
  non-party lists at municipal level. Formation rules, minimum members
  (eligible voters), filing.
- Joint lists (*yhteislista*) — two or more associations / parties
  combining for allocation purposes. Statutory mechanics.
- Electoral alliances (*vaaliliitto*) — how they differ from joint
  lists.
- D'Hondt application within and across alliances.
- Why Finland has no formal threshold and what natural threshold
  emerges from typical municipal magnitudes.
