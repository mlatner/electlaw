# Spain — local elections

Local elections governed by the *Ley Orgánica del Régimen Electoral
General* (LOREG, 1985). Closed-list D'Hondt. Crucially for our purposes,
LOREG explicitly recognizes **agrupaciones de electores** — voter
groupings — as an alternative path to the ballot alongside registered
parties and federations.

## Open questions to resolve

- LOREG articles governing *agrupación de electores* formation:
  signature requirements (scaled to municipality size), deadlines,
  filing officer.
- Equal-treatment provisions vs. registered parties.
- Funding regime: are *agrupaciones* covered by *Ley Orgánica de
  Financiación de los Partidos Políticos*, or by a separate rule?
- Practical incidence: in which municipalities have *agrupaciones*
  actually won seats, and against what kinds of opponents?
- The 5% threshold — applied at municipal level how exactly?

## Primary sources to acquire

- LOREG, Title III (local elections) and the general provisions on
  candidacies (Title I, Chapter VI).
- *Junta Electoral Central* doctrine on *agrupación* registration.
