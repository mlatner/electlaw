# Brazil — local elections

*Vereador* (city councilor) elections under the *Código Eleitoral* and
the *Lei das Eleições* (Lei 9.504/1997). Open-list PR with party or
coalition lists. Brazil is included less as a non-partisan-list model
and more as a stress test: the system is formally party-centric but the
intra-list seat assignment is so candidate-centric that it functions
nearly as a non-partisan competition within each list.

## Open questions to resolve

- Recent reforms to the *quociente eleitoral* and *quociente
  partidário* — current statutory formula.
- The 2017 abolition of *coligações* for proportional elections
  (Emenda Constitucional 97) — effects on small-party and local-list
  viability.
- *Federações partidárias* as a partial replacement — formation rules
  and effects.
- Whether any structure analogous to a non-party list exists for
  *vereador* elections, or whether registered-party affiliation is
  effectively mandatory.
- The *cláusula de desempenho* (performance clause) as it applies (or
  does not) at municipal level.

## Primary sources to acquire
