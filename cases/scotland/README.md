# Scotland — local elections (STV)

Local council elections under the *Local Governance (Scotland) Act 2004*
have used STV in 3- and 4-member wards since 2007. We include Scotland —
even though STV is otherwise out of scope — because the **administrative
infrastructure for grouping candidates** (party labels on the ballot,
ballot ordering rules, registration of "descriptions" for non-party
candidates) is directly relevant to how a US jurisdiction would handle
non-partisan groupings under any list-like ballot.

## Why this matters for the project

- Scotland shows what list-administration looks like when the underlying
  conversion rule is *not* a list formula. Candidates are still grouped
  visually and registered jointly, even though votes are individual
  rankings.
- It is a comparison for "what *can* be borrowed from list-PR
  administrative practice without importing list-PR vote conversion."
- The interaction between STV and **independents / non-party groupings**
  is the most documented in the UK.

## Open questions to resolve

- Ballot ordering rules: alphabetical within ward, Robson rotation

- Practical incidence of independent and "Independent–[label]"
  candidacies and how voters perceive those groupings.
