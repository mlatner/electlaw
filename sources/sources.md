## Foundational (defines the project)

### Charles Hamilton Houston Institute / Guinier Project — *Getting from Votes to Seats: A Primer on Electoral System Design*
- Local PDF: `sources/pdfs/Guinier_Project_Getting_from_Votes_to_Seats.pdf`
  (21 pages, downloaded 2026-05-04)
- Publisher: Charles Hamilton Houston Institute for Race & Justice,
  Harvard Law School
- Use in framework: **the conceptual foundation of the project.**
  Defines the three primary components — Seat Product (Assembly
  Size × District Magnitude), Ballot Structure, Allocation Formula
  — and supplies the project's terminology (Threshold of Exclusion,
  coordination problems Level A and Level B, restrictive ↔
  permissive spectrum, Hamilton/Jefferson/Webster naming alongside
  Hare/D'Hondt/Sainte-Laguë). The implementation guide produced by
  this project is the primer's much larger and more detailed
  follow-up.

### Germany — federal level

#### Primary statutes

- **Bundeswahlgesetz (BWahlG)** — *Federal Elections Act*. Current
  consolidated text 23 July 1993, last amended 7 March 2024
  (BGBl. 2024 I Nr. 91). <https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bwahlg/>
- **Official English translation** of the BWahlG, from the Federal
  Returning Officer (Bundeswahlleiterin). 42 pages. Cached at
  `sources/germany/Bundeswahlgesetz_EN_official.pdf`.
- **Bundeswahlordnung (BWO)** — *Federal Electoral Regulations*,
  the implementing instrument authorized by BWahlG § 52. Sets
  detailed rules on ballot form (§ 45), polling-station
  operations, vote counting (§ 69), and result establishment.
  <https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bwo_1985/>
- **Parteiengesetz (PartG)** — *Political Parties Act*. § 2
  defines "Partei" (party); §§ 18–31a govern public funding,
  donations, and disclosure.
  <https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/partg/>
- **Wahlprüfungsgesetz (WPrüfG)** — *Law on the Scrutiny of
  Elections*. 21 sections governing how Bundestag election
  results may be contested. Implements Article 41 GG and BWahlG
  § 49.
  <https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/wahlprg/>
- **Grundgesetz** (Basic Law) Articles 21, 38–41 — constitutional
  anchor for federal electoral law and political parties.

#### Constitutional decisions

- **BVerfG** judgment of 30 July 2024, 2 BvF 1/23 et al.
  (*Wahlrechtsreform 2023*). Held: Zweitstimmendeckung
  constitutional; abolition of *Grundmandatsklausel* renders the
  5% threshold currently unconstitutional under Articles 21(1)
  and 38(1) GG; *Grundmandatsklausel* applies transitionally.
  English press release:
  <https://www.bundesverfassungsgericht.de/SharedDocs/Pressemitteilungen/EN/2024/bvg24-064.html>
  Full ruling:
  <https://www.bundesverfassungsgericht.de/SharedDocs/Entscheidungen/DE/2024/07/fs20240730_2bvf000123.html>
- **BVerfG** ruling of 22 January 2025. Upheld unchanged signature
  requirements for new parties despite shortened early-election
  timelines.
